NIGHTROUTE UPDATE — INDIA-WIDE SEARCH, ROUTE OPTIONS AND COACH TYPES

- Removed the incorrect default that appended Karnataka to every unknown location.
- Location lookup now searches across India with Nominatim and tries Photon as a secondary fallback; known sample localities remain available offline.
- Requests up to three OSRM alternative road paths.
- Each available path produces AC Sleeper and Non-AC Seater demo variants with distinct illustrative service brands and different fare rates.
- Adds approximate intermediate route waypoints along the displayed map path. These are explicitly not verified bus stops.
- Rejects journeys above the 2,000 km road-distance cap when the road service can establish that distance, and provides a split-journey message.
- Animated bus markers continue to move along route geometry based on simulated schedule progress.

DATA HONESTY
Bus brands, service numbers, departure/arrival times, availability and fares are demo examples. Bus movement is simulated, not GPS. Real operator schedules, prices, service IDs and live tracking require authorized operator/aggregator APIs and GPS/AVL feeds. A village absent from map databases cannot be geocoded automatically; users may need to add its district or nearest mapped town.
